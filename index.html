Fair questions.

Is this just another to-do list?+

No. A to-do list just hands you a pile. HitGoals decides when each thing happens based on your actual week, and moves things around the moment that week changes.

Do I have to plan everything myself?+

Nope. Tell Arrow what you want to achieve and what your life looks like. It helps turn that into a plan you can actually follow.

What if my schedule suddenly changes?+

Your plan can change with it. Tell Arrow what happened, and it automatically moves things around.

Can I work on more than one goal at once?+

Yes. HitGoals helps balance your goals with everything else you already have going on.

What if I don't even know where to start?+

That's kind of the point. Talk it through with Arrow, and it helps figure out what your next steps should be.
